SIEM Detection Engineering, Alert Triage & SOC Operations

          
An end-to-end SOC lab: security telemetry flows into a central SIEM, custom detections are mapped to MITRE ATT&CK, alerts are triaged the way Tier-1 and Tier-2 analysts do, and validated threats go through incident-response playbooks.

          
ChallengeTurn raw security telemetry into reliable detections instead of noisy alerts.
ActionBuilt the SIEM architecture and correlation rules for brute force, lateral movement, persistence and exfiltration, mapped to MITRE ATT&CK, plus a phishing-response SOAR playbook.
ResultA SOC dashboard tracking MTTD and MTTR, with alerts triaged the way Tier-1 and Tier-2 analysts do. Team capstone, in progress.
